Texas 42 is a domino trick-taking game played with a double-6 set (28 dominoes). Four players form two teams: You & Partner vs. West & East.

Bidding: Players bid points they expect to win (30–42). High bidder names trump. Miss your bid and the other team scores it.

Tricks: Lead any domino. Others must follow suit if able. Highest pip of the led suit wins—unless trump is played.

Count dominoes: 5-5 (10pts), 4-6 (10pts), 0-5 (5pts), 1-4 (5pts), 2-3 (5pts). Each trick worth 1 pt.

Winning: First team to 7 marks wins!
